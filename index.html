    Questions

    What founders ask us first.

    What is AI search visibility?

    It's how often AI assistants such as ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Claude and Google AI name your product when buyers ask them which tool to choose. The work of improving it is often called generative engine optimization, or GEO.

    How is this different from SEO?

    SEO gets you ranked in a list of links. GEO gets you named inside the answer a buyer reads. They share signals, so we strengthen both, but we measure success by recommendations and pipeline.

    Which AI engines do you cover?

    ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Claude, Google AI Overviews and Google AI Mode. We track each one separately because they draw on different sources.

    How do you measure results?

    Share of voice across a fixed set of buyer prompts, citation rate per AI engine, AI-referred traffic, and the signups and demos that buyers attribute to an AI assistant.

    How long does it take?

    Expect 60 to 90 days for meaningful movement. You see progress against your baseline from the first monthly report.

    Can you guarantee ChatGPT will recommend us?

    No. No agency can force a model to name a product, and anyone who guarantees the top spot is guessing. We strengthen every signal AI uses to decide and show you the real answers every month so you can judge the change yourself.

    Do you write the content?

    Yes. We draft, your team approves, and nothing ships without your sign-off.

    We already have an SEO agency or content team. Does this replace them?

    No. We work alongside them. We own the AI layer: prompt mapping, citations, comparison pages, structured data and AI attribution, and we hand your team clear briefs for anything they publish.

    We're pre-launch. Is this for us?

    Usually not yet. GEO works best once you have a live product in a category buyers already ask AI about. Book a call and we'll tell you honestly.

    Who do you work with?

    Only software startups: B2B SaaS and AI products from seed to Series B. Focusing on one kind of buyer journey is how we stay sharp at it.
