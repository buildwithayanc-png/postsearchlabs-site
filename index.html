Questions

What people ask us first.

What is AI search visibility?

It's how often AI assistants such as ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Claude and Google AI name your business when people ask them for a recommendation. The work of improving it is often called generative engine optimization, or GEO.

How is this different from SEO?

SEO earns you a place in a list of links. GEO earns you a mention inside the answer people actually read. The two share many signals, so we strengthen both, but we measure success by recommendations and the customers they bring in.

Which AI engines do you cover?

ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Claude and Google AI Overviews and AI Mode. We track each one separately because they draw on different sources.

How long does it take to see results?

Expect 60 to 90 days for meaningful movement. You see the real AI answers in a report from the first month, so you always know where you stand.

Can you guarantee that ChatGPT will recommend us?

No. No agency can force an AI to name a business, and anyone who guarantees the top spot is guessing. We strengthen every signal AI uses to decide and show you the real answers every month so you can judge the change yourself.
