How is this different from SEO?

SEO gets you ranked in a list of links. GEO gets you named inside the answer a buyer reads. They share signals, so we build both, but we measure success by recommendations and pipeline.

How do you measure results?

Share of voice across a fixed set of buyer prompts, citation rate per AI engine, and the traffic, signups and demos that come from AI sources.

How long does it take?

Expect 60 to 90 days for meaningful movement. You'll see progress against your baseline from the first monthly report.

Yes. We draft, your team approves, and nothing ships without your sign-off.
