How we prove it

AI traffic hides in your analytics. We make it visible.

Many buyers read an AI answer, then type your URL or search your name. Default analytics files that under "direct". So we measure three ways and report all three.

1. Share of voice

A fixed set of buyer prompts, run on every engine each month. You see exactly which answers name you and which name a competitor.

"best [category] for [use case]" → named on 4 of 6 engines

2. AI referrer tracking

We set up your analytics to separate visits that arrive from AI assistants, and tag the links we can control.

chatgpt.com · perplexity.ai · gemini.google.com · claude.ai

3. Self-reported source

One field on your signup or demo form catches what analytics can't: buyers who heard about you from an AI assistant.

How did you hear about us? → "ChatGPT / an AI assistant"

Fit check

We're a good fit for some startups. Not all.

A great fit if you…

  • Sell a live B2B SaaS or AI product with paying customers
  • Are seed to Series B, competing with bigger incumbents
  • Are in a category buyers already ask AI about
  • Have someone who can review and approve content each week
  • Want a channel you can measure, not a vanity score

Probably not a fit if you…

  • Haven't launched yet, or have no clear category
  • Want a guaranteed #1 spot in ChatGPT
  • Want results by next week
  • Are looking for fake reviews or mass AI-written content
  • Aren't a software company. We work only with startups.

What we promise, and what no one can.

No agency can force a model to recommend a product. Anyone who guarantees the top spot in ChatGPT is guessing. What we can do is strengthen every signal AI uses to decide, and prove the change with the real answers every month.

And we won't take shortcuts that get brands burned: no fake reviews, no hidden text aimed at AI crawlers, no thousand-page AI content farms.

  • We show you the live AI answers before you spend a dollar.
  • Every page is approved by your team before it ships.
  • Reports built on real AI answers and real pipeline.
  • Works alongside your existing SEO and content team.
  • Clear scope and timelines agreed before any work starts.
