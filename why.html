What changed

There's no page two in an AI answer.

For twenty years, ranking on page one was enough. Now buyers skip the links and ask an AI assistant which tool to buy. It reads the web for them and hands back a short list. The tools on that list get the demo. Everyone else never learns they were considered.

Lost before the demo

Buyers compare vendors in ChatGPT, pick three, and contact only those. If you weren't named, the deal never shows up in your CRM.

Incumbents get named by default

AI leans on the brands mentioned most across the web. Bigger, older competitors win the answer even when your product is the better fit.

Content that ranks but never gets quoted

Your team ships posts every week. They rank on Google, yet AI quotes none of them, because they weren't written to be quoted.

AI describes your product wrong

Old pricing, a feature you killed, the wrong category. When AI gets you wrong, buyers rule you out before they ever reach your site.
