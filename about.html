About us

We get startups onto the AI shortlist.

PostSearchLabs is a generative engine optimization (GEO) agency for B2B SaaS and AI startups. When a buyer asks ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Claude or Google AI which tool to choose, our job is to make your product one of the names in the answer, and to show you what that brings in.

Why PostSearchLabs exists

Software buyers used to search Google, open ten tabs and compare vendors on their own. Now many of them ask an AI assistant something like "best CRM for a 20-person sales team" or "alternatives to a tool they already know", and the answer names two or three products.

That short answer often decides who gets the demo. A startup can have the better product and still never be considered, because it isn't in the answer and there is no page two. Most agencies still report rankings and clicks. We started PostSearchLabs to measure and improve the thing that now writes the shortlist: whether AI recommends you.

What we do

We work on every signal AI assistants rely on before they recommend a tool:

  • Buyer prompt map. The recommendation, alternatives and "X vs Y" questions buyers in your category actually ask.
  • Share-of-voice baseline. Those prompts run across six AI engines: ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Claude, Google AI Overviews and Google AI Mode, each tracked separately.
  • Citation gaps. The review sites, listicles, communities and docs AI cites for your category, and where you're missing.
  • Entity and schema fixes. A consistent product description, structured data, llms.txt and AI crawler access.
  • Comparison and alternatives pages. Answer-first content AI can quote, approved by your team before anything ships.
  • AI attribution reporting. Share of voice, citations, AI-referred traffic and the signups and demos buyers credit to an AI assistant.

GEO builds on SEO rather than replacing it. The same work usually strengthens your Google rankings too, and we work alongside any SEO agency or content team you already have.

Who we work with

Only software startups: B2B SaaS and AI products from seed to Series B, with a live product and paying customers, competing with bigger incumbents for the same buyers. Categories include AI tools, vertical SaaS, dev tools, fintech, HR and payroll, and sales and marketing tech.

Focusing on one kind of buyer journey is how we stay sharp at it. If you're pre-launch, it's usually too early, and we'll tell you so.

How we work

  • Real answers, not a made-up score. Every report shows what the AI engines actually said about your category.
  • No guaranteed top spot. No agency can force a model to name a product, and AI answers vary from one run to the next. We raise how often you're named and make it visible, so you can judge the change yourself.
  • You approve everything. We draft, your team signs off, and nothing ships under your name without it.
  • Tied to pipeline. Visibility only matters if it turns into signups and demos, so that's what we report on.

Common questions

What is PostSearchLabs?
A generative engine optimization (GEO) agency for B2B SaaS and AI startups. We get startups recommended when buyers ask AI which tool to choose, and tie that to pipeline.

Why only software startups?
Software buyers follow a similar path: ask AI for the best tools, compare alternatives, then book demos. Focusing on that one journey lets us go deeper on the prompts, sources and pages that decide it.

Is GEO the same as SEO?
No. SEO gets you ranked in a list of links. GEO gets you named inside the answer a buyer reads. We strengthen both, but measure success by recommendations and pipeline.

How long does it take?
Expect 60 to 90 days for meaningful movement, measured against your baseline from the first monthly report.
