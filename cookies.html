Cookie policy

Last updated 22 November 2026

Cookies are small files a website saves in your browser. This page lists the cookies postsearchlabs.com can set, what each one does, and how to change your choice. Only necessary cookies are used until you say otherwise.

Turn on JavaScript to see or change your cookie choice.

Your choices

The first time you visit, a banner asks whether to allow analytics and marketing cookies. "Reject all" is as easy as "Accept all", and "Customize" lets you pick each category. You can change your mind at any time with the "Cookie settings" link at the bottom of every page. When you turn a category off, we delete the cookies it set on this site and stop loading those tools. We ask again after 6 months, or sooner if we add a new tool.

Necessary

These keep the site working. They're always on and don't track you.

These help us see which pages are useful and where visitors come from, including visits from AI assistants. They only load if you allow Analytics.

We don't use any analytics tools right now. If we add one, it will only load after you turn Analytics on, and we'll list it here.

Other services

Our fonts come from Google Fonts and our pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. Neither sets cookies here, though both receive your IP address to deliver the files. "Book a call" opens Cal.com in a new tab, where Cal.com's own policy applies.

Clearing cookies yourself

You can also delete or block cookies in your browser settings. If you delete the psl_consent cookie, the banner will ask you again on your next visit.
