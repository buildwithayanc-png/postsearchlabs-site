Privacy policy

Last updated 22 November 2026

This policy explains what information PostSearchLabs ("we", "us") handles when you visit postsearchlabs.com or book a call with us. We keep it short because we collect very little.

Who we are

PostSearchLabs is an AI search visibility agency founded and run by Ayan Chakraborty.

This website

The only form on this site that sends us your details is the AI visibility check on our home page, described below. Analytics and marketing cookies are only set if you allow them in our cookie banner, and you can change that choice at any time with the "Cookie settings" link at the bottom of every page. We don't use an analytics provider right now. If we add one, we'll name it in our cookie policy, which lists every cookie we can set, and it will only load after you allow Analytics. The free shortlist test and AI Shortlist Scorecard run entirely in your browser: what you type or select is not sent to us or stored. If you open a prompt in an AI assistant, that assistant's own privacy policy applies. Like every website, our hosting provider (GitHub Pages) processes basic technical data such as your IP address and browser type in order to deliver pages and keep the service secure. Our fonts are served by Google Fonts, which also receives your IP address when the page loads.

Booking a call

When you book a call, the booking is handled by Cal.com. You give them your name, email address and anything you choose to write in the booking form, and they share it with us so we can hold the call. We use it only to prepare for and run the call and to follow up with you about it.

The AI visibility check form

When you fill in the AI visibility check form, we receive your company website, your name and work email, your role, your company stage, a one-line description of your product and any competitors you list. We also record the page you sent it from, the site that linked you to us, any campaign tags in the link (such as utm_source) and your browser's time zone, so we know how you found us and when to reach you. The form is stored in our database, which is hosted by Supabase. We use it only to review your site, reply to you and follow up about your enquiry. We don't add you to a mailing list.

Working with us

Our work uses your company's public information: your website, docs, review-site profiles, listings and public mentions. Where we set up analytics or form tracking for you, we work in your own accounts and never ask for your customers' personal data.

Sharing

We don't sell or rent personal information. We share it only with the services needed to run the business (such as Cal.com, Supabase and our email provider) or when the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep booking details, form submissions and emails for as long as we have a business relationship with you, and delete them when you ask us to. Your cookie choice is remembered in your own browser for 6 months.

Your choices

You can ask us to show, correct or delete the information we hold about you at any time. Mention it on your call or reply to any email from us, and we'll take care of it.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page. Our terms cover how we work with clients.
